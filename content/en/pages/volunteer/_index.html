Want to become a part of the No To Big Tech movement? Find out how you can get involved here.

body: >-


For the No To Big Tech movement to be a success it needs your help. We've listed some of the ways in which you can contribute to the movement below. If you don't find anything suitable, but want to follow along anyway, you should sign up for our newsletter below.


SOME OF THE TASKS WE CURRENTLY NEED HELP WITH:
 Hosting webinars across non European time zones and in different languages (Spanish, French, etc) - but more English speakers are also welcome to host webinars if they wish to.
 Helping to translate materials (website content, webinar slides).
 Welcoming volunteers and general admin tasks.
 Researching local opportunities to talk about Big Tech. Task list last updated on 29/10/2026

Of course - if you have another skill or idea to present to the movement, please tell us about it.


If you are interested in any of the above - or just want to register your interest in contributing to the movement - please complete the form below. If you have any issues, you are welcome to email us.


This is a form for people to register their interest in supporting with the DeGoogle movement.Note - if you want to help by hosting webinars, we will discuss this with you and can provide some basic training/guidance on what a good workshop should contain.All you need to do is to show people how to shift from a specific Google product to a recommended ethical alternative, eg how to migrate from Gmail to Protonmail.   Field 1 = Name Field 2 = Your time zone Field 3 = How would you like to help? Field 4 = Optional - for those who want to help, but aren't sure how / want to suggest something. Field 5 = 

Any other thoughts, comments, advice or feedback on the DeGoogle project

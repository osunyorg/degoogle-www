We're a grassroots digital activist group that is fighting back against Big Tech. We are an openly anti-capitalist group of citizens who are here to discuss and co-ordinate a strategic response against Big Tech and AI. We aim to help raise awareness of the problems with Big Tech and AI, and to highlight the obvious role these forms of tech play within capitalism, colonialism and imperialism. 


There are many well documented issues with Big Tech:

NO PRIVACY

ANTI HUMAN RIGHTS

FUELS INEQUALITY

BUILT-IN ADDICTION

NO TRANSPARENCY

NO ACCOUNTABILITY

AVOIDS TAX

FUELS HATRED

FUELS CONSUMPTION

DAMAGING TO MENTAL HEALTH

TARGETS CHILDREN

DIGITAL COLONIALISM

SUPPORTS GENOCIDES

ALIGNED WITH AUTHORITIAN REGIMES

THREAT TO DEMOCRACY

MONOPOLISTIC

SURVEILLANCE CAPITALISM

WHITE SUPREMACY


We need to ditch Big Tech collectively & support more ethical, people-powered and people-owned tech.

We need to apply pressure at the political level to heavily regulate and break up the  Big Tech companies.

We need to apply pressure to the businesses that proudly support Big Tech.
We need to critically analyse the role that digital tech is having upon society at the local level.
